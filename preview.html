Approach

A different approach to web work.

Kiroku is built around a simple idea: a website should not be a fragile black box. It should be understandable, documented, version-controlled where appropriate, and easy to keep current.

The aim is not just to make websites look better, but to make them calmer to operate — more resilient, more maintainable, and more useful over time.
