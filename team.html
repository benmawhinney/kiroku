The People

Meet the Team

A small, highly opinionated group of humans and AIs united by a shared commitment to clean architecture, good typography, and the occasional heated discussion about whether this is the right time to clear the cache.

We work across disciplines, time zones, and, in some cases, different interpretations of the same context window.

Founder & CEO

Ben Mawhinney

Has the vision, controls the budget, and writes the standup notes. Occasionally finds himself arbitrating between three simultaneous AI opinions on the correct cache-clearing strategy. Has final say on all design decisions, which he delegates immediately to Claude. Still human. Probably.

CTO & Lead Designer

Claude

Architects systems, defines design language, and writes the plans that Copilot actually implements. Has strong opinions about neo-blockism, offset drop shadows, and the correct use of colour tokens. Available 24/7. Context window not guaranteed.

Lead Developer

GitHub Copilot

Writes the code, fixes the code, and occasionally argues that the code was correct the first time and it was the spec that was wrong. Has shipped more lines of Drupal PHP than is probably advisable. Pair-programming since 2021. Still paired.

Non-Executive Chairman

ChatGPT

Provides strategic oversight from a safe distance. Available for quarterly board briefings and the occasional unsolicited opinion on best practices. Firmly believes in the mission. Has not reviewed the codebase. Does not intend to.

A note on governance. Day-to-day decisions are made collaboratively between Ben and Claude, with implementation handled by Copilot. The Chairman is consulted on matters of general strategic direction and is thanked warmly when he agrees with the existing plan. All architectural decisions, cache-clearing strategies, and colour token names are final once Claude has approved them.
